METHODS / SAFEGUARDS

Context, not accusation.

One publishable population

Districts, divisions, departments and weeks all use the same publishable lower-level records. Raw report coordinates, IDs and descriptions never enter these products. Medians are computed from pooled eligible records.

Privacy threshold: 5

Any rare department × week × rolling-window cell withholds its whole upazila across every view. Monetary and outcome fields have additional withholding. Unsafe amount bands withhold the entire band table.

Spatial statistics

Global and Local Moran and Gi* use publishable counts, Queen adjacency and seed 42. Local labels exclude islands. Results are exploratory, not corrected for multiple comparisons and never evidence of wrongdoing.

Coverage and denominators

These are published reporting patterns, not estimated prevalence. Unknown dates are all-time only. Population rates and bias ratios need a complete, cited denominator matching the boundary version; otherwise they remain unavailable.

Privacy suppression is not differential privacy. Comparing releases can disclose changes; an owner and privacy review is still required before live republication. The synthetic preview is not an observed dataset.

The 2020 geoBoundaries ADM3 release has 544 lower administrative polygons, including urban units; it is not a claim that Bangladesh currently has 544 upazilas. Parent membership follows the pinned district crosswalk.
